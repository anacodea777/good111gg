Privacy Policy: good111gg

Last updated: 16 November 2026

good111gg ("the app") is a simple coin flip app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device data. It has no account sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it does not request internet access or any device permissions.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only your settings (language, sound on/off) and your flip tally (number of heads and tails) on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
